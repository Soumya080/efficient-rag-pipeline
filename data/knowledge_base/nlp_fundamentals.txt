Natural Language Processing (NLP) enables computers to understand, interpret, and generate human language.
A crucial first step is Tokenization, where text is split into smaller units (tokens) such as words or subwords (like BPE or WordPiece).
Tokens are then mapped to dense vector representations called Embeddings (e.g., Word2Vec, GloVe, or Contextual embeddings from BERT).
Language modeling is the task of predicting the next word in a sequence. Modern NLP heavily relies on pre-trained language models fine-tuned on specific downstream tasks like sentiment analysis or question answering.
