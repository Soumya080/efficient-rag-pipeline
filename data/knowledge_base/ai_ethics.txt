AI Ethics is a crucial field addressing the moral implications and societal impact of artificial intelligence.
A major concern is Algorithmic Bias, where AI systems inherit and amplify human prejudices present in their training data, leading to unfair outcomes in areas like hiring or criminal justice.
Interpretability and Explainability are also vital, especially in high-stakes domains like healthcare, where users need to understand why an AI made a specific decision.
Other ethical considerations include privacy, job displacement, accountability, and the long-term safety of advanced AI systems.
